Privacy policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

What Spog collects

Spog collects the photos you take in the app, the country you spot in, and the city name when you enable location. Nothing else. No advertising identifier, no behavioural tracking, no third-party analytics.

Your photos

Photos are stored on your device. To identify a vehicle, a photo is sent to our server, which forwards it to an artificial-intelligence provider for analysis, then returns the result. Before any transmission, licence plates detected in the image are blurred on your device. Transmitted photos are not used to train any model and are not kept once the answer is returned.

Country and location

Location is optional and the app works fully without it. When enabled, your position is converted immediately into a country code and a city name, then discarded. No coordinates are stored or transmitted, and no movement history is built. Permission requested is "while using the app" only.

No account

Spog requires no account, no email address and no password. Your garage lives on your device. If you delete the app, everything it contains is deleted with it.

Third parties

Only vehicle identification involves a third party, and only for the photo being analysed. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising purposes. Apple handles subscription payments; we never see your payment details.

How long data is kept

Your cards and photos stay on your device for as long as you keep them. Removing a card from the garage deletes its photos immediately and permanently.

Your rights

Since no data is linked to an identity on our side, we hold nothing to give you access to or to delete on request. Everything is in your hands, on your device. You may withdraw location permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.
