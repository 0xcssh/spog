Purpose

Spog is a game. You photograph cars you come across in real life; each one becomes a collectible card in your garage. Rarity, points and levels are game mechanics with no monetary value and cannot be exchanged or resold.

Access

Spog is open to anyone aged 13 or over. Using the app requires a compatible device and, for identification, an internet connection.

Road safety

Never use Spog while driving. Above a certain speed, the app locks scanning by itself whenever your location is on. Do not shoot from a moving vehicle, do not step into the road, and respect other people's privacy: photograph cars, not people. You alone are responsible for the circumstances in which you take a photo.

Fair play

Catches must come from the live camera, aimed at a real vehicle. Photographing a screen, a magazine, a poster or a scale model is against the rules. We may remove cards obtained this way.

Subscription

Some features require a paid subscription, billed through your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it at any time from Settings, or from your Apple Account. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased.

Identification accuracy

Vehicle identification relies on automated analysis and can be wrong. When a model is missing from the catalog, it may be added automatically from what the analysis read, so its name may be approximate. Rarity depends on the country you spot in and is an editorial choice, not an official ranking. Neither is guaranteed.
